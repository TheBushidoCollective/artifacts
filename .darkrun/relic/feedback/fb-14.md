FIT-1. Three routed decisions have no owner in any unit body and no completion criterion anywhere in the station.

`publish.md` §6 and `service.md` §7 route twelve decisions between them, and **no unit carries a sweep criterion for either section**. `design-container-and-crypto` criterion 10 sweeps `format.md` §4; `design-topology-and-origins` criterion 11 sweeps `viewer.md` §7. Nothing sweeps `publish.md` §6 or `service.md` §7, so those items are covered only where a unit body names them. Three are named in no body:

1. **`publish.md` §6.2, whether proof of work is in the flow and at what difficulty.** Grep of all five unit files for "proof of work" and "proof-of-work" returns zero hits. This is the primary anti-abuse control available to a no-accounts service. `design-storage-grant-and-cost` §2 decides "the per-IP publish quota," which is the alternative instrument, so the station can close having chosen the alternative by omission while the routed decision sits open. `publish.md` §6.2 also states the consequence: "if difficulty is non-zero: `service.md` gains one grant-time refusal code." Deciding this after `shape` closes means reopening a locked spec document.

2. **`publish.md` §6.4, the retry cap and the backoff bounds.** Zero hits for "retry cap" or "backoff" in any unit. `publish.md` §6.4 states the consequence: "when `upload_retries_exhausted` fires and how much egress a single failing publish can consume." That is egress, which is `design-storage-grant-and-cost`'s cost domain, and `upload_retries_exhausted` is an error code already fixed in a locked document. Storage is told to read `publish.md` §4 but is never told to decide this.

3. **`service.md` §7.7, the mint dedup interval.** Zero hits for "dedup" in any unit. `service.md` §2.2 makes this load-bearing on cost, not ergonomics: a repeat inside the interval "does consume the cap," and "the worst-case egress arithmetic in `docs/preconditions.md` collapses if a mint returns a usable URL without consuming it." The value sits in the seam between `design-topology-and-origins` §4, which decides when the mint fires, and `design-storage-grant-and-cost` §2, which decides the cap. Each can reasonably assume the other owns it. `service.md` §7.7 also notes it "interacts with the frame's 120-second window."

**What would make this pass:** assign each of the three to a named unit with a body instruction that states the decision and its consequence, and add a sweep criterion of the form already used twice in this station ("every decision routed here by `publish.md` §6 / `service.md` §7 is decided with its consequence stated, or explicitly eliminated with the reason") to whichever units take them. Proof of work and the mint dedup interval are the two that ship as holes into `build` with a locked-document consequence attached.
