FIT-7. Completion criteria that a unit can satisfy in full while leaving the risk unbounded.

Most criteria in this station are strong, and the four headline irreversibles each have a dedicated one (container 5 through 8 for the framing, topology 5 for the origin split, topology 8 for the mint trigger, storage 11 for the project topology). Three gaps sit outside that coverage.

**1. `design-operations-and-abuse` §5 has no criterion at all.** The section is titled "Two leaks and one non-issue, **none of which any document owns**," which makes it the highest-value gap-closing work in the unit, and criteria 5 through 11 never touch it. A worker can satisfy every criterion and drop all three items: cross-relic correlation and where it is disclosed, whether the viewer distinguishes cap exhaustion from takedown, and recording enumeration as settled so a later station does not relitigate it as a reason to shorten IDs. The third is explicitly forward-looking protection for `build`, and nothing checks that it was written. Add a criterion naming all three.

**2. `design-topology-and-origins` §5, edge fidelity, has no criterion.** The body states the consequence precisely: "the natural default is in the valid set and choosing it violates a locked rule, because it makes an MCP client prompt users to sign in against an authorization server that does not exist," plus which status the edge cannot emit. This is `service.md` §7.1, a routed decision with a locked-rule violation attached, and criteria 5 through 12 cover the download origin, the PSL, HSTS, the mint, Open Graph, quotes, the `viewer.md` §7 sweep, and dashes. Nothing covers it.

**3. Several criteria test that a sentence exists rather than that the decision is safe, and two of them matter.** Storage criterion 7, "The grant decision is written as a decision tree keyed on probe outcomes, so no leaf is left undecided," is satisfiable by a tree with a leaf reading "use the signed PUT" even where `publish.md` 3.6's "computed against the cap rather than the declared size" is, in the unit's own words, "not implementable as written on this branch." The criterion checks tree shape, not whether each leaf states what it breaks in a locked document and routes it as drift. That routing is the whole point, since the alternative is `build` discovering a locked sentence it cannot implement. Similarly, container criterion 8, "states the v2 migration cost in bounded terms," is satisfied by an assertion; what bounds it is the mandatory TTL, and the TTL ceiling is storage's decision in a unit container cannot read. Container can write a bounded-sounding claim resting on a number nobody has picked yet.

**What would make this pass:** a criterion on operations §5 naming its three items; a criterion on topology §5 naming the rate-limit deny status decision and the status the edge cannot emit; and on storage criterion 7, a requirement that every leaf state which locked-document sentence it contradicts, if any, and route it as drift to its owner.
